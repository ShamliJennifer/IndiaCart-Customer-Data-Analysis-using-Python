The **IndiaCart Customer Data Analysis** project uses Python to understand customer information and purchasing behavior.

## 💡 Business Insights

Some important patterns identified from the analysis include:

* Most customers are in the **30–50 age range**.
* The **East region** has the highest average monthly salary.
* Male and female customers have relatively similar spending patterns.
* Monthly salary has some **very high values**, which are outliers.
* Many customers have **Bachelor's or Master's degrees**.
* Customer spending is fairly similar across different demographic groups.

These insights can help businesses better understand their customers and make data-driven decisions.
